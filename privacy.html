隐私政策Privacy Policy

本政策说明 RES Financial Model 在本网站与软件许可过程中如何处理信息。提供方：Yellowfir Group Limited。 This policy explains how RES Financial Model handles information on this website and during software licensing. Provider: Yellowfir Group Limited.

1. 我们收集什么What we collect

  • 许可验证：申请许可证时提交的邮箱、订单号；软件运行时的设备标识（用于绑定许可证）与许可状态心跳。 Licence verification: the email address and order number submitted when requesting a licence; a device identifier and licence-status heartbeat sent while the Software runs (used to bind the licence).
  • 支付信息：由 Paddle 直接收集与处理，我们不存储您的卡号。 Payment information: collected and processed directly by Paddle. We do not store your card number.
  • 网站统计：可能使用匿名访问统计（页面访问量），不含个人身份信息。 Website analytics: anonymous page-view statistics may be used; these contain no personally identifying information.

2. 不收集什么What we do not collect

本软件不上传您的模型参数、计算结果或任何项目数据；所有计算均在本机完成。 The Software does not upload your model parameters, results or any project data. All calculations are performed locally on your machine.

3. 用途How we use it

仅用于：许可证签发与校验、订单与退款处理、必要的服务通知（如版本更新或许可到期提醒）。 Only for: issuing and validating licences, processing orders and refunds, and essential service notices (such as version updates or licence-expiry reminders).

4. 共享Sharing

仅为完成支付与许可，与 Paddle（支付）及云服务商（许可服务托管）共享必要信息；不向第三方出售数据。 Necessary information is shared only with Paddle (payments) and our cloud provider (licence-service hosting) to complete payment and licensing. We do not sell data to third parties.

5. 保留与删除Retention and deletion

许可与订单记录保留至许可期限届满后 2 年。您可随时邮件要求删除个人数据（法律法规要求保留的除外）。 Licence and order records are retained for 2 years after the licence term ends. You may request deletion of your personal data by email at any time, except where retention is required by law.
